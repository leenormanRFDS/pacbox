Construction is complete on the Flying Doctor’s new $21 million centre, designed to increase primary health care access and grow the health workforce across Far North SA.

When the centre opens

  • A bulk-billed GP and nurse-led primary health service, increasing access to multidisciplinary primary health care.
  • Placements for junior doctors and medical, nursing and allied health students in partnership with education providers.
  • Fit-for-purpose School of the Air facilities supporting educational services for isolated children.

With thanks

$1 million seed funding from BHP Copper SA and $1 million from the South Australian Government’s Enabling Infrastructure Program. The balance was financed through RFDS fundraising and community donations.
